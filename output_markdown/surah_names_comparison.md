against the unique transliterations and names extracted from `output_markdown/volume_1/Ihya_Ghazali_Eng_Book_volume_1_Complete_complete.md`.

## Summary Statistics

- **Total Surahs in Quran:** 114
- **Surahs Cited in Volume 1:** 90 (78.9%)
- **Surahs with Differences in Transliteration/Naming:** 65
- **Surahs with Exact Matching Transliteration Only:** 25
